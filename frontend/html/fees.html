Fees and Pricing

Effective Date: July 19, 2025

At SmartStock, we aim to provide a flexible and affordable inventory management platform accessible to all. Our current pricing is structured to accommodate both individual users and growing small businesses.

1. Free Tier (Starter Plan)

  • ✔ 100 inventory items
  • ✔ 1 user account
  • ✔ Basic reporting and multilingual support
  • ✔ Email support
  • Price: Free forever

2. Premium Plan (Coming Soon)

  • ✔ Unlimited inventory items
  • ✔ Multiple user accounts (team access)
  • ✔ Advanced analytics and notifications
  • ✔ Offline mode and custom reports
  • ✔ Priority support
  • Estimated Price: $3.99/month (subject to final release)

3. Payment and Refunds

Currently, SmartStock is available for free use during the public beta phase. Once premium plans are introduced, payment terms will be provided via a secure platform. Users will be notified in advance before any charges are applied.

4. Changes to Pricing

We reserve the right to adjust our pricing based on feature updates, user feedback, or operational needs. Any changes will be communicated at least 14 days in advance via email or on the website.

5. Questions?

Please contact us at billing@smartstock.app with any billing-related inquiries.
